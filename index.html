About

I am a translational neuroscientist with over 20 years of experience bridging basic research and drug development. My career has spanned prestigious academic institutions including Harvard Medical School and the University of Cambridge, as well as leadership roles in the pharmaceutical industry.

As former Scientific Director at a multinational preclinical CRO, I led international teams delivering neurophysiological biomarker programmes for major pharmaceutical companies and innovative biotech firms. My expertise centres on EEG methodology, pharmaco-EEG profiling, and the design of translational studies that de-risk CNS drug development.

I hold a PhD in Clinical Medicine and have authored over 20 peer-reviewed publications in leading journals including Brain, Sleep, and Neurotherapeutics. My research has been supported by over £270,000 in competitive funding.

EEG Biomarker Strategy & Analysis

Development and validation of EEG-based biomarker strategies for CNS drug programmes. Expert analysis of quantitative EEG data, spectral power, coherence, and event-related potentials to support regulatory submissions and go/no-go decisions.

  • qEEG biomarker identification and validation
  • Spectral power and coherence analysis
  • Event-related potential (ERP) methodology
  • Regulatory-grade biomarker reporting
  • Go/no-go decision support for drug programmes

Pharmaco-EEG Study Design

End-to-end design of pharmaco-EEG studies from protocol development through to data interpretation. Dose-response characterisation, sleep-wake profiling, and seizure liability assessment in preclinical and translational models.

  • Protocol development and SOP creation
  • Dose-response characterisation
  • Sleep-wake state profiling and scoring
  • Seizure liability and pro-convulsant risk assessment
  • Data interpretation and scientific reporting

Preclinical-to-Clinical Translation

Strategic guidance on bridging preclinical findings to clinical development. Translational biomarker selection, cross-species comparison of neurophysiological endpoints, and evidence-based recommendations for first-in-human study design.

  • Translational biomarker selection and qualification
  • Cross-species neurophysiological endpoint comparison
  • First-in-human (FIH) study design recommendations
  • Preclinical-to-clinical gap analysis
  • Regulatory strategy and IND/CTA support

AI/ML in Neuroscience

Application of machine learning and artificial intelligence approaches to neurophysiological data analysis. Automated signal processing, pattern recognition in EEG datasets, and development of predictive models for drug response classification.

  • AI/ML strategy consulting for biomarker programmes
